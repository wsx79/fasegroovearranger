Fase Groove Arranger is installed!

STANDALONE APP
- Start it from the Start menu and choose your audio output in AUDIO.
- All three Fase Groove engines are included. No other DAW or Fase Groove plugin is needed.

FIRST STEPS
1. Choose a genre, key and progression, then press NEW ARRANGEMENT.
2. Press PLAY. Click a track name for its instrument panel, or double-click a clip to edit MIDI.
3. SAVE your project and EXPORT the mix or separate tracks as WAV, MP3 or MIDI.

UPDATES
OPTIONS -> SETTINGS -> CHECK NOW checks and downloads the latest version.
Save your project, then choose INSTALL NOW. The setup keeps the installation folder and preferences.
Your projects, samples, exports and autosaves are not removed by uninstalling.
