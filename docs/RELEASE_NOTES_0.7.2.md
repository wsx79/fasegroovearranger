## Musical regeneration fixes

Generate song and regenerate track, section or clip now create persistent musical takes, rather than mainly changing the feel of the same riff. The selected bass figure develops chord-safe answers and pickups. Track motifs remain coherent through sections, and drum stems generated together share the kit motif.

RND LANE now draws a new take in instrument panels. APPLY TRACK/CLIP applies current controls without rerolling. Your chosen instruments, sounds, mix, automation, locks and clips outside the requested scope are preserved. Fixed presets, custom grids and explicit riff seeds remain intentional constraints.

Includes the family Windows installer, portable Windows/macOS/Linux packages and integrated updates from 0.7.1. Projects saved by earlier releases remain readable; your existing MIDI changes only when you regenerate it.

Validation: all thirteen genres tested for musical phrase diversity without counting velocity or humanisation, scope isolation, deterministic takes and save/reload. Native core/IO/updater tests, Windows installation/upgrade/uninstall, UI/editor checks, unchanged-sound audio regeneration comparisons and the full thirteen-genre club/audio audit.
