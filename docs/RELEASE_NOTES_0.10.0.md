## Piano nel genere, pannelli e 0.10.0

Piano random riffs, variations, breaks and build-ups stay in the song genre. Mix fills, accents and random progressions follow gospel, jackin', garage, afro, funky, neo soul and chill. House-family grooves keep their figures. The song genre drives this; there is no second genre menu in the engine panel.

Engine panels no longer repeat the section menu, the track-volume fader or the piano `.fgapreset` button. Tempo, key, transport and export stay on the arranger. Saved parameters are unchanged.

Also includes the piano, drum and guitar engine fixes landed after 0.9.0. Projects saved by earlier releases remain readable.

Includes the family Windows installer and portable Windows, macOS and Linux packages.
